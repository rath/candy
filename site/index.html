From your terminal.
At home on your Mac.

Preview an image in a Kitty-compatible terminal, directly or inside tmux. Check a file’s format, size and colors without opening it. Need an edit? Open a native macOS window sized to the image, with familiar tabs, shortcuts and save dialogs. 

$ candy photo.png
$ candy inspect photo.png
$ candy edit photo.png
# Your shell is ready for the next command.
