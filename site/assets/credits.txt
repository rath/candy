candy-demo-en.mp4 and candy-demo-ko.mp4: Real Candy 0.2.1 AppKit windows, built locally from the release code and recorded on 2026-10-11 with ScreenCaptureKit, using a display capture that excludes every other window, in the light appearance and with the English or Korean interface chosen for that process alone. Scripted pointer moves, drags and clicks drove the edits; every mosaic and highlight was applied by Candy, and no application UI was generated or composited. Idle pauses were shortened, playback runs about 1.9 times faster than recorded, and the three scenes are joined with crossfades on the coral stage colour. H.264, 1280 x 1100, 30 fps.
candy-editor-en.png and candy-editor-ko.png, with WebP encodings at quality 88: the finished order-page scene, captured from the same window with macOS screencapture and scaled to 1280 x 1100. They are the videos' posters, the reduced-motion images and the social-card sources.
Demo scenes: a fictional order page, group chat and spreadsheet, written as HTML in scripts/demo/scenes/ and rendered with system fonts in a visible Chromium browser at 2x. Every name, address, email address (example.com), phone number (010-0xxx in Korean, the 555-01xx range reserved for fiction in English) and account number is invented. No photographs or generated imagery.
og-en.png and og-ko.png: Authored English/Korean social card layouts, rendered at 1200 x 630 in a visible Chromium browser from scripts/og/index.html using scripts/build-og.sh. They combine the language's real Candy editor still (candy-editor-en.png or candy-editor-ko.png), the Candy mark (mark.svg), and live HTML/CSS typography in the system font, including the product name. These localized cards are used for social previews; no application controls were generated or altered.
mark.svg and favicon.svg: Original vector lollipop artwork authored for Candy, sharing the spiral of the app icon sources in scripts/icon/. The favicon is the same mark on a transparent background, scaled to fill the icon box. No third-party or generated imagery.
GitHub corner in index.html and ko/index.html: the triangle and octocat paths are from Tim Holman's "GitHub Corners" (MIT, https://github.com/tholman/github-corners), inlined with the site's own colors and animation rules.
