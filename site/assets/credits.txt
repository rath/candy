candy-editor.png: Real Candy 0.2.0 AppKit window captured locally with macOS screencapture on 2026-10-11 at 2080 x 1678 (Retina), in the dark appearance, after the window sized itself to the image at Actual Size. Capture source: .impeccable/review/site-editor-0.2.0.png. The headline highlight (yellow, 88 px, 40%) and email mosaic (22 px blocks) were applied in Candy; application UI is not fabricated or composited. Only PNG metadata and lossless compression were changed after capture.
candy-editor.webp: WebP encoding of the same capture at quality 86, for the website. PNG remains the lossless fallback and social-card source.
og-en.png and og-ko.png: Authored English/Korean social card layouts, rendered at 1200 x 630 in a visible Chromium browser from scripts/og/index.html using scripts/build-og.sh. They combine the same real Candy editor capture, the Candy mark (mark.svg), and live HTML/CSS typography in the system font, including the product name. These localized cards are used for social previews; no application controls were generated or altered.
mark.svg and favicon.svg: Original vector lollipop artwork authored for Candy, sharing the spiral of the app icon sources in scripts/icon/. The favicon is the same mark on a transparent background, scaled to fill the icon box. No third-party or generated imagery.
GitHub corner in index.html and ko/index.html: the triangle and octocat paths are from Tim Holman's "GitHub Corners" (MIT, https://github.com/tholman/github-corners), inlined with the site's own colors and animation rules.
Inside the app: weekend-demo.png, a 2000 x 1320 fictional travel card rendered by scripts/make-fixtures.swift. Its coastal artwork was generated with OpenAI image generation and is committed as scripts/fixture-assets/coast.jpg; the original prompt and provenance are in scripts/fixture-assets/README.md. Typography and all traveler/booking details are synthetic. No personal data or third-party stock photography.
