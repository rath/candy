candy-editor.png: Real Candy AppKit window captured locally with Peekaboo on 2026-10-11 at 1800 x 1400 (Retina). Capture source: .impeccable/review/fixture-editor-final.png. og-en.png and og-ko.png: Authored English/Korean social card layouts, rendered at 1200 x 630 in a visible Chromium browser from scripts/og/index.html using scripts/build-og.sh. They combine the same real Candy editor capture, the Candy mark (mark.svg), and live HTML/CSS typography. These localized cards are used for social previews; no application controls were generated or altered.
favicon.svg and mark.svg: Original vector lollipop artwork authored for Candy, sharing the spiral of the app icon sources in scripts/icon/. No third-party or generated imagery.
Inside the app: weekend-demo.png, a 2000 x 1320 fictional travel card rendered by scripts/make-fixtures.swift. Its coastal artwork was generated with OpenAI image generation and is committed as scripts/fixture-assets/coast.jpg; the original prompt and provenance are in scripts/fixture-assets/README.md.
